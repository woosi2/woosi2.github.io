I am a Master's student in the Department of Chemistry at Sogang University.

My research interests lie in **soft matter**, **statistical thermodynamics**, and **molecular simulation (MD/MC)**. I am particularly interested in understanding the structural and thermodynamic behavior of complex molecular systems through computational methods.

Currently, my research focuses on lipid membranes and vesicles, including membrane equilibration, lipid organization, and curvature-dependent properties.
