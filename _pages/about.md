I am a Master's student in the Department of Chemistry at Sogang University.

My research interests lie in **soft matter**, **statistical mechanics**, and **molecular simulation (MD/MC)**. I am particularly interested in understanding the structural and dynamical behavior of complex systems, such as lipid membranes, polymers, electrolytes, and metallic glasses. This is why I am drawn to molecular simulation and fascinated by statistical mechanics, which allow me to explore the microstates of these systems and connect them to macroscopic structural and dynamical properties.

Currently, my research follows two directions:

- **Lipid vesicles.** Using coarse-grained MARTINI simulations, I study how membrane curvature induces asymmetry in cholesterol flip-flop dynamics and phase behavior in DPPC/cholesterol vesicles. I found that the inner and outer leaflets can exhibit different phase behavior depending on cholesterol concentration, revealing an asymmetric phase response across the curved membrane. Building on this finding, I am extending my research to more complex ternary vesicles known to form raft-like domains. To investigate phase separation in these systems under well-defined equilibrium conditions, I am currently developing a vesicle equilibration methodology based on hybrid Monte Carlo–molecular dynamics (MC-MD).
- **Metallic glasses.** I train machine-learning interatomic potentials for Zr–Ni–Al metallic glasses and use them to relate local atomic structure, such as icosahedral clusters and their networks, to deformation and fatigue.
